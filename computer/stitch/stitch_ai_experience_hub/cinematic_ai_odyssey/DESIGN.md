## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a premium, cinematic discovery portal designed to showcase artificial intelligence across five curated thematic universes. It targets tech-forward creators, researchers, digital explorers, and enterprise innovators who value high-fidelity interactive media and seamless exploratory interfaces.

The aesthetic fuses **Cinematic Minimalism** with **Refined Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Rich, abyss-like spatial backdrops paired with multi-layered translucency evoke deep cosmos and neural intelligence.
- **Luminescent Energy:** Selective chromatic accents (violet, cyan, gold, and vibrant orange) act as focal beacons, punctuating complex discovery spaces without visual fatigue.
- **Restrained Precision:** Clear, structural layouts and disciplined Scandinavian-inspired typography ground the futuristic aesthetic into an authoritative, tactile experience.

## Colors
The color palette relies on deep abyssal dark tones to create an endless canvas, amplified by crisp glowing nodes and semantic universe signatures.

### Base Palettes
- **Canvas Base (`#0B1020`):** Root background, providing a deep-space visual anchor.
- **Surface Elevation (`#151E31`):** Subordinate panels, navigation docks, and secondary viewports.
- **Card Surface (`#1A2740`):** Elevated interactive tiles, media containers, and modal sheets.
- **Text Dominance (`#F8FAFC`):** Primary display headings, active state labels, high-legibility inputs.
- **Text Subdued (`#AAB8D1`):** Metadata, descriptive body, non-active states, hairline dividers.

### Thematic Universe Signatures
Each universe harnesses a dedicated kinetic aura and interactive border system:
1. **Data & Fremtid (Data & Future):** Cyan (`#4DD9E8`) — Algorithmic logic, cold intelligence, high precision.
2. **Visuel Magi (Visual Magic):** Violet (`#9B7CFF`) — Latent space, generative dreams, aesthetic synthesis.
3. **Viden & Læring (Knowledge & Learning):** Gold (`#F0C36A`) — Scholarly enlightenment, crystalline clarity.
4. **Spil & Opdagelser (Games & Discoveries):** Vibrant Orange (`#F28A3C`) — Dynamic play, energy, responsive agency.
5. **Kreativitet & Teknologi (Creativity & Technology):** Prismatic blend of Violet (`#9B7CFF`) transitioning to Cyan (`#4DD9E8`).

## Typography
The typographic architecture juxtaposes structural futurism with modern Danish functional legibility:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Engineered, geometrical, and decisive. Accents Danish typographic rhythm with balanced proportions and distinctive character shapes.
- **Body & Long-form (`Manrope`):** Ultra-refined geometric sans-serif delivering balance, open counters, and high readability against low-luminosity backgrounds.
- **Labels & System Meta (`JetBrains Mono`):** Computational, mono-spaced precision used for universe metrics, parameters, latency indicators, and taxonomies.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column dynamic fluid grid designed for widescreen spatial exploration, condensing smoothly down to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices.

### Grid Orchestration
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` canvas margins. Deep horizontal stage areas allow multi-universe carousels and multi-tiered interactive dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 6-8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Side-by-side comparative views shift to tabbed or staggered vertical cards.
- **Mobile (&lt;768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen edge margins. Discovery feeds stack into continuous single-column streams with full-bleed touch zones.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved using ambient volumetric lighting, glassmorphism, and colored edge illumination rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Translucency:** Primary card components use backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over `#1A2740` with an alpha opacity of `75%` to `85%`.
- **Ghost & Luminescent Borders:** Containers rely on a dual-layer hairline outline: a foundational 1px inner border (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.08)`) combined with an external directional glow corresponding to the respective universe's color at `20%` to `40%` opacity on hover or focus.
- **Ambient Field Shadows:** Deep-space elevation uses spread, colored ambient shadows: `0 12px 40px -10px rgba(11, 16, 32, 0.8)` layered with subtle radial back-glows matching the universe theme.

## Shapes
A balanced roundedness (`0.5rem` base, scaling to `1rem` on cards and `1.5rem` on prominent preview panels) pairs with precise linear cuts.

- **Component Standard:** Modals, cards, and floating control groups use `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to keep the interface approachable yet engineered.
- **Pills & Selectors:** Filter chips, universe state badges, and micro-actions adopt full pill radials (`rounded-full`) to differentiate metadata from structural bounding boxes.
- **Media Cutouts:** High-fidelity video and interactive 3D viewports utilize clipped `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) contours with recessed glass bevels.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid glowing gradient matching the active universe (e.g., Orange `#F28A3C` gradient to Gold for Games; Violet `#9B7CFF` to Cyan for Visuals). High-contrast `#0B1020` or `#F8FAFC` text, `0.5rem` border-radius, accompanied by a dynamic outer aura on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px var(--universe-glow)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Translucent `#151E31` fill with 1px border `rgba(170, 184, 209, 0.25)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`. Shifts to pure accent border and brightened fill on hover.

### Discovery Cards & Universe Tiles
- **Structure:** Encased in glass-filtered `#1A2740` with `1rem` corner rounding and subtle 1px border.
- **Interactive State:** Mouse position tracking activates a localized spotlight overlay (`radial-gradient`) in the universe's signature hue, illuminating metadata, contextual tags, and an active edge highlight.

### Chips & Universe Tags
- **Appearance:** Built with `label-code` typography, displaying universe categorization and metadata badges.
- **Styling:** Pill-shaped, minimal background `rgba(21, 30, 49, 0.8)`, framed by a 1px border tinted in the corresponding universe color.

### Form Inputs & Query Fields
- **Appearance:** Surface background `#151E31` with `#F8FAFC` text and `#AAB8D1` placeholder text.
- **Focus Mechanism:** Hairline border transforms from `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.1)` to the universe accent color with an accompanying soft `4px` blur focus ring.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- **Base Style:** Dark container (`#151E31`) with 1px border (`#AAB8D1`).
- **Active State:** Checkboxes and radio discs fill with the active universe hue, featuring crisp `#0B1020` iconography and a high-saturation localized halo.

### Universe Switcher Dock (Specialized Component)
- **Appearance:** Persistent floating navigation bar docked at the viewport bottom or header, rendered with heavy frosted glass (`blur(24px)` over `rgba(21, 30, 49, 0.75)`).
- **Segments:** 5 discrete universe nodes with micro-indicators that glow when selected, transitioning layout theme tokens dynamically across the hub.
